Scientific Directions

Core Research Pillars

Integrating advanced macromolecular engineering, micro/nanofabrication, and clinical translational biomaterials.

01

Hemostatic Dressings & Advanced Hydrogels

Development of fast in situ injectable hydrogels reinforced with Montmorillonite (MMT) and Laponite nanoclays for lethal noncompressible torso hemorrhage; electrospun PCL/chitosan oligomer hemostatic membranes; and marine polysaccharide tissue adhesives.

Nanoclay Electrospinning Torso Hemorrhage Curcumin Delivery
02

3D Tissue Engineering & Acellular Matrices (dECM)

Pioneering acellular extracellular matrix (dECM) scaffolds isolated from squid skin and yellowfin tuna skin for regenerative wound care; 3D printed Triply Periodic Minimal Surface (TPMS Gyroid) scaffolds for stem cell proliferation and oral/maxillofacial bone tissue engineering.

dECM Scaffolds 3D Printing TPMS Stem Cells Skin Regeneration
03

Nanofibers, Aerogels & Smart Flexible Sensors

Large-scale fabrication of functional micro/nanofibers via Centrifugal Spinning (Forcespinning®); porous lightweight polyimide aerogels for extreme thermal insulation; and conductive PEDOT:PSS nanofibrous aerogels for piezoresistive pressure sensors and environmental separation.

Centrifugal Spinning Polyimide Aerogels Flexible Sensors Oil-Water Separation
